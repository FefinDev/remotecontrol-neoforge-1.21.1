Workflow

Three clicks.

+
STEP 1

Link

Hold Shift and right-click a Control Block. The remote saves its coordinates and chimes once. Any other block is rejected.

→
STEP 2

Switch

Right-click in the air or on any block. The remote checks the saved block and flips it.

STEP 3

Power

The Control Block lights up and holds strength 15 on all faces until you flip it again.
